About ZenBook Platform

ZenBook is a next-generation Cyber Safe Social Media Platform designed to combine the best of social networking with cutting-edge safety mechanisms.

Our platform integrates intelligent hate speech detection that automatically monitors user comments and messages. When toxic or vulgar content is detected, users receive immediate warnings, and after three violations, protective measures are implemented to maintain community standards.

With dedicated modules for Admins, CyberCell officers, and Users, ZenBook ensures a multi-layered approach to online safety while preserving the social experience users expect.
